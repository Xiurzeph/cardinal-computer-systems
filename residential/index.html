Quick Diagnostics & FAQs

Have an immediate issue? Review these quick diagnostics or trace your system symptoms below.

How do I determine if my Wi-Fi router is failing? +
If you notice packet drops while standing close to your ISP router, or your smart home systems disconnect randomly, your internal DHCP table may be congested, or the local environment requires structured access points to route around interference.
What is your diagnostic and payment policy? +
We pride ourselves on direct transparency: We operate a No-Fix, No-Fee local standard. If we diagnose an issue and determine that it cannot be fixed safely under standard parameters, you do not pay a dime.
How does remote technical support operate safely? +
We use completely secure, fully encrypted remote desktop tunnels. You approve the handoff, you watch our engineers diagnostic steps in real-time, and once the session finishes, the portal is permanently severed, meaning your machine cannot be reached without a new direct handshake.
